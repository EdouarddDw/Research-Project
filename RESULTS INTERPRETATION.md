Summary

For Area 1 (Overfitting Detection): the model inflates PDP range for noise feature x5 from 0.1 to 0.4. It also invents phantom interactions, with fake pair x2 × x9 scoring H=0.85 while real pair x1 × x2 scores only H=0.05. Real interactions are learned early and stabilize, while fake ones appear late and keep growing. Several real interactions (x1 × x2, x2 × x3, x3 × x4) are barely learned or forgotten by late epochs, likely because model capacity is consumed by spurious patterns.
For Area 2 (Interaction Directionality): we found a Crossover interaction at x0 × x1, where the conditioned PDP curves cross and the direction of x0's effect reverses depending on x1. We found a Modifier interaction at x2 × x3, where the curves fan out but do not cross, meaning x3 changes only the strength of x2's effect. The log-group interactions (x3, x4, x6, x7) are too weak for the model to capture with this setup.

The most useful methods turned out to be H-stat evolution for distinguishing real vs fake interaction timing, PDP range for detecting overfitting through importance inflation, interaction signatures for classifying Modifier vs Crossover, and clustered ICE for revealing heterogeneity that PDP averages away.
